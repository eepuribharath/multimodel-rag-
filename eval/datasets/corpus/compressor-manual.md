# Compressor XK-42817 Service Manual

## Torque specifications

The maximum torque for compressor part XK-42817 is 5.5 Nm. Exceeding this
value voids the warranty and risks cracking the intake housing. Use a
calibrated torque wrench; hand-tightening is not acceptable for final assembly.

## Maintenance schedule

The XK-42817 requires a full service every 400 operating hours. Service
includes replacing the intake filter (part XK-F220), inspecting the impeller
for pitting, and re-greasing the main bearing with lithium-complex grease.

## Storage

Store the unit horizontally in a dry environment between -10°C and 45°C.
Drain condensate before any storage period longer than two weeks.
