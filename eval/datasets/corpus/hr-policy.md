# Employee Handbook — Leave and Onboarding

## Annual leave

Full-time employees accrue 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
prorated in the first year from the start date. Unused days up to a maximum
of 5 may be carried into the next year and must be used by March 31.

## Requesting leave

All leave requests go through the HR-9 portal and require manager approval
at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days.

## Probation

New hires are on probation for 6 months. During probation, the notice period
is one week on either side.
