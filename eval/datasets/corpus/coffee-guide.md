# Espresso Brewing Guide

## Core parameters

Brew espresso at 9 bar of pressure with water at 93°C. Aim for a brew ratio
of 1:2 — for an 18 g dose, target 36 g of espresso out in 25 to 30 seconds.

## Grind

Adjust grind finer if the shot runs faster than 20 seconds, coarser if it
chokes past 35 seconds. Change one variable at a time.

## Milk

Steam milk to 60–65°C. Above 70°C the sweetness drops sharply because the
lactose begins to break down.
