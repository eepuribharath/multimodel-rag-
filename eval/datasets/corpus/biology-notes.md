# Cellular Respiration — Study Notes

## Where ATP is made

Most of a cell's ATP is produced in the mitochondria, specifically across the
inner mitochondrial membrane by ATP synthase during oxidative phosphorylation.

## The Krebs cycle

Each turn of the Krebs cycle directly yields 1 ATP (as GTP), 3 NADH, and
1 FADH2. Since one glucose molecule feeds two turns, the direct yield is
2 ATP per glucose — the big payoff comes later in the electron transport chain.

## Anaerobic fallback

Without oxygen, cells fall back to fermentation, which nets only 2 ATP per
glucose and produces lactate in muscle tissue.
