# Q3 Financial Summary — Internal Memo

## Topline

Q3 revenue came in at $4.2M, up 22% year over year, driven primarily by the
enterprise tier. Net revenue retention held at 118%.

## Costs

Operating expenses fell 12% quarter over quarter after the infrastructure
migration completed in July. Largest remaining line item is cloud spend at
$310K per month.

## Runway

At the current burn rate the company has 19 months of runway, which extends
to 24 months under the conservative hiring plan.
