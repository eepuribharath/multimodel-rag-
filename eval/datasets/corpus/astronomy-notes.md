# Notes on the Jovian Moons

## Europa

Europa's outer ice shell is estimated to be 15 to 25 km thick, covering a
subsurface ocean that may hold twice the water of all Earth's oceans
combined. Surface temperature averages about -160°C at the equator.

## Io

Io is the most volcanically active body in the solar system, driven by tidal
heating from its orbital resonance with Europa and Ganymede.

## Ganymede

Ganymede is the largest moon in the solar system and the only one known to
generate its own magnetic field.
