# IT Onboarding Checklist

## Accounts

New starters get SSO credentials on day one; the identity provider is
Okta tenant corp-eu-2. MFA enrollment must be completed within 48 hours.

## Hardware

Standard issue is a 14-inch laptop, model LPT-M4-14. Engineers may request
the 16-inch LPT-M4-16 with manager approval. Hardware refresh cycle is 3 years.

## Access requests

Production access requires a JIRA ticket of type ACC-PROD plus approval from
the service owner. Access is reviewed quarterly and expires after 90 days
unless renewed.
