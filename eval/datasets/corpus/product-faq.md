# AURA-500 Air Purifier — FAQ

## Warranty

The AURA-500 carries an 18-month limited warranty covering the fan motor,
control board, and sensors. Filters are consumables and are not covered.

## Returns

Unopened units may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Opened units are subject to a 15% restocking fee.

## Filters

Replace the HEPA filter (SKU AURA-F13) every 6 months under normal use, or
every 3 months in homes with pets.
